Chawan — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Chawan is a personal log for matcha sessions. It is private by design: everything you record stays on your device. Chawan has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of its own — the developer cannot see your data because it never leaves your device.

What Chawan stores, and where

  • Your sessions and tins (matcha brands, grades, amounts, ratings, prices, free-text notes, dates) and app settings are stored on your device only.
  • Tin photos stay on your device only. They are never uploaded and never leave the device through Chawan.

No accounts, nothing collected

Chawan does not offer account creation and does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information — no email address, no identifiers, nothing. There is no data about you for the developer to hold, sell, or lose.

Analytics & tracking

Chawan contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. It does not collect a device identifier, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not profile you.

Sharing

Share cards you create are generated on your device as an image and handed to the system share sheet only when you choose to share. Chawan does not upload them or post anything on your behalf.

Your control & data deletion

  • Delete any session or tin inside the app at any time.
  • Export a full backup (JSON) of your sessions and tins from Settings whenever you like.
  • Deleting the app deletes your data with it — nothing remains anywhere else, because nothing was ever sent anywhere else.

Children

Chawan is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “last updated” date above will change with it. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
